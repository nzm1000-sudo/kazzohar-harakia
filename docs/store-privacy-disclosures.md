# Store Privacy Disclosures

Draft for final store forms. Recheck against the native build.

## Apple App Store Connect

- Data linked to the user: none currently intended.
- Tracking: no.
- Account creation: no.
- Location: precise location is optional and used for zmanim, calendar, timezone, and place labeling after user action. Do not describe it as background location.
- Diagnostics, analytics, advertising, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, health, payments, and push notifications: none in the current product core.
- Privacy policy URL: pending a stable public HTTPS URL.

## Google Play Data safety

- Data collected by the developer: no server-side account or analytics collection in the current product core.
- Device-local storage: preferences, progress, favorites, and cached app resources.
- Location: optional approximate/precise device location may be transmitted to the listed third-party services only when the user chooses location features.
- Data shared with third parties: request data needed by Hebcal, Sefaria, Nominatim, TimeAPI, and occasional Commons image loading.
- Security: HTTPS is used for the listed external services; local storage is controlled by the platform.
- Deletion: clear local app/site data; no developer-held account record exists.

These bullets are preparation notes, not the final legal or console submission text.
