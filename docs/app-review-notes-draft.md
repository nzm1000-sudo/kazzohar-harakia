# App Review Notes Draft

כזוהר הרקיע is an independent Hebrew RTL daily-use reference and learning app for zmanim, calendar, prayer, Tehillim, halacha source study, and Talmud reading.

The app has no login, account, subscription, in-app purchase, ads, analytics, or social posting. Core preferences and learning progress remain on the device. Location is optional: reviewers can use manual city search without granting location permission. If location is tested, tap the explicit location action and allow it; the app uses it for zmanim, date context, timezone, and place labeling.

The app reads selected texts and schedules from Hebcal and Sefaria over HTTPS. Source details, edition metadata, attribution, license information, and external links are available in the relevant reader. The product is independent and is not an official Sefaria, Koren, or Steinsaltz product.

Offline behavior: the app retains its local shell and local progress, while remote text, calendar, location, and image content requires network access. No account or special reviewer credentials are required.

Final submission must replace this draft with exact build-specific instructions and confirm that all displayed editions and images are cleared for distribution.
