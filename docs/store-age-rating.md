# Store Age Rating Draft

This is a conservative preparation note, not a submitted rating.

- Category: reference, education, or books, subject to the final store taxonomy choice.
- Audience: general audience; do not use Kids Category metadata unless the product is redesigned and reviewed for that category.
- No gambling, purchases, ads, social accounts, chat, public posting, user-generated content, violence, sexual content, or medical diagnosis features were found in the current core.
- Religious and halachic material should be described accurately and without claims of official authority or professional advice.
- Review the sensitive halacha topics and external source content for the final catalog and choose the store questionnaire answers from the actual displayed content.
