# Jastrow — deterministic abbreviation extractor

Source: Jastrow 1903 (Sefaria, public domain; `sources/jastrow/raw/entries.jsonl.gz`, already cleared). Extractor:
`scripts/dictionary/lexica/abbreviations.mjs` → `extractJastrowAbbreviations`. Output (regenerated by every build):
`sources/jastrow/generated/abbreviations.tsv` (abbreviation, expansion, profile, sourceEntry, rawPattern, confidence,
status).

## Patterns (all entries, 32,512)
- `«PHRASE» (abbr. «X», «Y»)` and `(abbrev. …)` — the Hebrew phrase printed before the parenthesis is the expansion.
- `(abbr. «X» = «Y»)` — the reordered reading (אא״ב = בשלמא אי אמרת); the one whose letters fit is kept.
- A headword followed by its own `(abbrev. «X׳»)` (אֲפִילּוּ → אפי׳).
- Inside the phrase, the headword's letter-plus-geresh (א׳ העולם under אוּמָּה) is replaced by the headword; such a
  reading is marked `substituted` (confidence MEDIUM): the phrase may inflect it (א׳ העולם is אומות העולם), so it only
  corroborates another source.
- `abbr. of X` (a shortened proper name: אבטילוס) is not an acronym and is skipped. English is never read.
- Every reading passes the letter check (`initialsFit`): the abbreviation must spell the beginnings of the expansion's
  words in order (one function word may be skipped), or be the start of the one word it cuts off (אפי׳ → אפילו).

Jastrow prints far fewer abbreviations than a dedicated dictionary: 239 "abbr./abbrev." mentions in all his entries.
