### Permission basis

Torat Emet's detailed redistribution terms identify books that are not marked as Wikisource works as the second license group. The terms list this work in the official book catalog and state that this group may be redistributed under `CC BY-NC-SA 2.5` for non-commercial use, with attribution and prominent disclosure of the license terms.

The app must therefore remain free and non-commercial for this content. The app must not imply endorsement by the author, rights holder, publisher, or Torat Emet.

### Required attribution and notice

Every Yalkut Yosef source screen and the app's license/source details must identify:

> קיצור שולחן ערוך ילקוט יוסף, הרב יצחק יוסף, מהדורת התשס"ז. מקור: תורת אמת.

The book-specific page also displays the notice `כל הזכויות שמורות (c) למו"ר הרב יצחק יוסף שליט"א` and requires that copied excerpts cite the source. That notice is retained as metadata and is not replaced by the corpus-level license statement.

The distributed app must provide a prominent link or equivalent accessible presentation of the `CC BY-NC-SA 2.5` terms, including attribution, non-commercial use, share-alike for adaptations, and disclosure of the license on redistribution.

The source was checked against the official Torat Emet catalog and official rights pages before import.
